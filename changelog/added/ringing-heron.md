Core: Experimental: a reader reads first and subscribes only when a read comes back empty, since a notification for a stream with no notifier is dropped. `SubscribeResponse.newest_counter` names the newest record after the attach landed, so a reader whose read stopped below it reads once more before it sleeps. `WaitForNotification` reads the stream once more just after it sends the poll, and answers at once for a record that landed before the notifier existed.
