Add experimental `temporalio.client.StreamStore`, the base class of a Stream Store, and `temporalio.testing.MemoryStreamStore`, a Store that keeps its Streams in memory for tests and local runs.
Add experimental Streams for client and Activity code. `stream_config=StreamConfig(stores=[...])` of `Client.connect` gives a client its Stream Stores, and `Client.create_stream` and `Client.get_stream` return a `Stream` to append to, read, describe and close. A failed call raises `temporalio.exceptions.StreamError` with a `reason`. A read of a Stream from `get_stream` needs `body_type=` or a `StreamRef` with a type argument, and raises `TypeError` with neither. `temporalio.contrib.streams.current_producer` gives the Producer of an Activity attempt, and `as_producer` gives back one that code stored.
