Experimental `temporalio.contrib.streams`: a standalone Activity owns a stream. In a standalone Activity, `activity_handle()` is the Activity's own stream, and it writes as `<Activity id>@<run id>`. `StreamRef.for_activity` opens it, a read ends when the Activity run ends, and `delete_activity_streams` deletes it.
Experimental `temporalio.contrib.streams`: `start_activity_with_stream` starts an Activity that owns a stream of its own. The Worker closes the stream when the Workflow sees the Activity end, and the server's notifier closes it after its graces when that close never comes.
Experimental `temporalio.contrib.streams`: an Activity's producer attempt is `started_unix_ms << 11 | min(attempt, 2047)`, from the time the server started the attempt and its Temporal attempt. A retry and an attempt after a reset start later, so they supersede the earlier attempt, and the key fits 53 bits.
