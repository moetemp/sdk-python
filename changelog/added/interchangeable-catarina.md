Core: Experimental: the Nexus `read` takes `floor_holder`. With it the read holds the floor and answers `replay`, the range of the records that crossed after the byte cut, with Core's digest over them. The new Nexus `read_range` operation, `NexusReadRange` for lang, reads that range again through the codec step and checks the digest. Its answer has no byte budget and carries only the records and `next_token`. It checks that the chain it names is one of the Workflow's.
