Experimental: `StreamReader` reads in a Local Activity through the transport `StreamDirectReads` maps its endpoint to, by default (`replay_from_store=True`). History keeps each read's range and digest, a few hundred bytes, in place of its records, and a replay reads the range again from the owner's store. A read with no transport fails at once with `StreamUnsupportedError`. `replay_from_store=False` records each answer in History and needs no transport.
