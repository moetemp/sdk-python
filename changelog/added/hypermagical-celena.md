Core: Experimental: behind `streams`, `streams::connect_stream_service` builds one stream store from a `StreamStoreConfig` (Redis or memory) and serves lang's stream calls in process: append, read, latest, close and owner delete, as serialized `coresdk.streams` requests. Its store goes to each Worker's `stream_store`, so a Workflow's output and outside producers share it.
