Decode a generic dataclass by a hint with type arguments, such as `Box[int]`, and a value with a transfer type by a hint such as `T | None`. A `StreamRef[T]` then keeps its type as a dataclass field, in a list, and as an argument or a result that can be `None`. A union with a generic dataclass member and a later member that takes the JSON, such as `Union[Box[int], dict]`, decoded to the later member and decodes to the generic dataclass. A top-level hint `X | None` for a class `X` with a transfer type decoded to the transfer type value, such as the proto message, and decodes to `X`.
